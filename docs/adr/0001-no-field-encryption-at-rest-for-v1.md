# No field-level encryption at rest for v1.0

The v1.0 trust differentiator ([positioning decision](https://github.com/lbacik/paysubscriptions/issues/22)) required deciding whether `User.email` and `Subscription` fields (name, monthly/yearly amounts) needed application-level encryption before launch, since today they're stored as plain columns. We decided **not** to add field-level encryption for v1.0: the amounts and subscription names are not especially sensitive under GDPR, and the engineering cost of introducing and key-managing encryption doesn't fit the solo-maintainer, low-operational-cost constraint on this effort. Instead, the v1.0 privacy policy must plainly disclose that data is stored unencrypted at rest, so the trust claim stays honest rather than implying protection that doesn't exist. See [Decide the minimum privacy/data-handling commitments backing the v1.0 trust claim](https://github.com/lbacik/paysubscriptions/issues/30).
