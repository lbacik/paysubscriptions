# Keep hosted Umami Analytics for v1.0, disclosed rather than self-hosted or removed

Umami Analytics (cloud.umami.is) loads on every page today with no consent banner and no privacy-policy disclosure. As part of deciding the v1.0 privacy/trust commitments ([issue #30](https://github.com/lbacik/paysubscriptions/issues/30)), we considered switching to self-hosted Umami (keeps analytics data off third-party infrastructure entirely) or dropping analytics altogether, against keeping the hosted service as-is. We decided to keep hosted Umami: it's cookieless and doesn't collect personal data, so no consent banner is legally required, and self-hosting is an operational cost that doesn't fit the solo-maintainer constraint on this effort. The v1.0 privacy policy must instead name Umami Cloud explicitly as a sub-processor receiving anonymized page-view data, so the "we don't share your data with third parties" claim ([issue #30](https://github.com/lbacik/paysubscriptions/issues/30)) doesn't silently exclude it.
